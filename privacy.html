Fourth Phase

Privacy Policy

Effective September 14, 2026

Fourth Phase does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

The game has no account system, advertising, analytics, tracking SDK, multiplayer service, or in-app purchase system.

Data stored on your device

Fourth Phase saves game progress, settings, Daily and Challenge results, and replay identifiers locally in the app's private storage. A bounded error report may also be stored locally when the app crashes. The game does not transmit this information; copying or sharing a report is your choice. Your operating system may include app data in a device backup if you enable that platform feature.

Network access

The game itself does not require a network connection. Opening this privacy policy or a shared challenge link uses your device's browser.

Retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until you reset it in the game, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Because the developer does not receive this data, there is no server-side player record to retrieve or delete.

Children

Fourth Phase does not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else.

Contact

Developer: CNRB Studios LLC. Questions about this policy can be submitted through the Fourth Phase support page or emailed to dombrown@cnrbstudios.com.
