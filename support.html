Fourth Phase

Support

Fourth Phase is a landscape football strategy game.

Quick fixes

  • Rotate your phone to landscape before playing.
  • Music and Sound Effects can be switched independently in Settings. If you are playing your own music, the game keeps it playing where the platform supports this.
  • Use Reduced Motion if reveal or celebration movement is uncomfortable.
  • Active saves are versioned and preserved when the game cannot safely read them. Keep the app installed while requesting help; uninstalling or clearing app storage can remove progress.

Contact

Email dombrown@cnrbstudios.com and include your device type plus what you were doing when the issue appeared. If the recovery screen appears, restart first; you can copy its error details and include them in your email if you choose.
